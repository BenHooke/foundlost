# Post types

## Public/Temporary

No login required, user can make a post at any time, but will not have all the benefits of a persistent account, this will make it more streamlined and attractive for new users. The user will be prompted with a form with the time's name, a description, and contact info, this can be a text field that they can put their email address or phone number in. This post will automatically expire after 30 days to keep the database/map clean.

## Private/Persistent  -  Do this later

Authentication required, this type of post will function very similarly to the temporary posts, but they will have the option to edit it at any time, extend it's life past 30 days if the item is still lost, and allow users to choose to communicate in-app instead of sharing their personal contact information. This will require us to implement a notification/messaging feature later on (Static messages, no websockets required)
